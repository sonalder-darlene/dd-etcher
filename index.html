Full Disk Access required

dd-Etcher needs Full Disk Access to read and write block devices. Without it, flashing will fail.

  1. Click Open System Settings below.
  2. Find dd-Etcher in the list and toggle it on.

dd-Etcher

Flash an image to a drive. Nothing else.
